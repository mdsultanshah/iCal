# Glossary

Words used in the appointments prototype.

- **Appointment**: a titled block of time on a single day. It can include a note. Every appointment has a start and an end.
- **Example**: a sample appointment added the first time you open the calendar. An Ex mark labels it. Clear examples removes only these.
- **Today**: the current day. A small dot sits under its number.
- **Selected day**: the day whose appointments are listed. In the month grid it is filled in black in the light theme, and inverted in the dark theme.
- **Time zone**: the time zone of this browser, shown once in the details column. Appointment times use that zone.
- **12h / 24h**: how times are written. 24-hour is the default. The choice is saved in this browser.
