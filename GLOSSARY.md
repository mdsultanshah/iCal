# Glossary

Words used in the appointments prototype.

- **Appointment**: a titled block of time on a single day. It can include a note.
- **Example**: a sample appointment added the first time you open the calendar. The Example label marks it. Clear examples removes only these.
- **Today**: the current day, marked on the calendar.
- **Time zone**: the time zone of this browser, shown once in the header. Appointment times use that zone.
