# Glossary

Words used in the calendar prototype.

- **Appointment**: a titled block of time on a single day. It can include notes. Every appointment has a start and an end.
- **Example**: a sample appointment added the first time you open the calendar. An Ex mark labels it. Clear examples removes only these.
- **Today**: the current day. Its number has a marker.
- **Selected day**: the day the keyboard is on. On a phone, its appointments are listed under the month.
- **12h / 24h**: how times are written on the calendar. 24-hour is the default. The choice is saved in this browser. The small add box always takes a 24-hour start.
